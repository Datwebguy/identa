Privacy

This is a static results page for a hackathon project. It has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no analytics of our own.

  • What the page stores: your light or dark theme choice, in your own browser (local storage). It never leaves your device.
  • What the host sees: the page is served by Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as your IP address and the pages requested. See Vercel's privacy policy.
  • What the results contain: counts and links taken from the project's own saved runs. They name public companies and public pull requests in a repository owned by the project's owner. They contain no personal data about visitors.

Terms: the page is provided as is, to show measured results. It is not a service and makes no promise of availability.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
